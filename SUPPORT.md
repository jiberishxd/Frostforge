# Help with JiberishUI

Start with [Getting started](docs/GETTING-STARTED.md), [provider compatibility](docs/ADDON-COMPATIBILITY.md), or [cast-bar setup](docs/CAST-BARS.md).

## Report a bug or fitting problem

[Open a bug report](https://github.com/jiberishxd/JiberishUI-WoW/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). Include:

- Your game client and build: Retail or Forever.
- JiberishUI version and the names/versions of the UI addons involved.
- The affected component, selected artwork and provider.
- What you expected, what happened, and steps to reproduce it.
- A screenshot of the frame and the relevant `/jui` settings; include `/jui status` output when possible.

The development preview has automated checks, but custom layouts and real-client behavior still need player feedback. Reports about unsupported frame types can be submitted as feature requests.

## Share an idea or layout

[Request an improvement or share a layout](https://github.com/jiberishxd/JiberishUI-WoW/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml). Describe the goal and the components/providers involved. Artwork feedback is most useful with a screenshot and a precise location: a clipped corner, a gap, an emblem or a fitting issue.

Use **Guide → Backup** before experimenting with a layout. Restore and Reset component are available in `/jui`; you do not need to delete your saved settings to reset artwork.
