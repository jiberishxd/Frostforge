Matching cast-bar borders

Painted health-style rails above and below the cast bar, with complete leaves, bindings and shaped ends extending freely outside it. Matching materials for every class, race and faction; a clear center for cast text and progress.

Subtle

A quiet painted edge with minimal weight.

Classic

Natural painted rails and freely extending details. Default style.

Bold

A little more presence; the ornaments keep their proportions.

Defaults to Match unit artwork. Choose any class, race or faction for the cast border separately if you prefer.

In game: /jui → Player, Target or Focus → Cast bar. Enable Cast-bar border, select a style and keep the provider on Automatic or choose Blizzard, EllesmereUI or ElvUI. Border width, height, weight and spacing are adjustable. The provider must have its own cast bar enabled.

This is an offline 180 × 16 UI-unit cast-bar example using pieces exported directly from the addon. Border only: native casts, channels, empowered stages, interrupts, timers and icons remain provider-controlled. Real client rendering still needs your in-game check.
